# Audio

## Priority order

1. Voice clarity
2. Intentional silence
3. Sound effects
4. Music

Music should never reduce narration intelligibility.

## Voice

Narration should sound natural and appropriately paced for the format.

Avoid:
- excessive speed
- monotone delivery
- overly dramatic delivery when the subject does not justify it

## Music

Music should support:
- pace
- tone
- tension
- reveal
- emotional continuity

Music does not need to be continuously loud.

## Ducking

Reduce music level under narration as needed.

## Sound effects

Use SFX to support:
- impacts
- transitions
- number reveals
- UI interaction
- scene changes
- important visual motion

Do not add SFX to every animation.

## Silence

Silence can increase impact before:
- reveal
- punchline
- important statistic
- emotional transition

## Sync

Important sound accents should align with exact visual events.

## Deliverables

Where practical, retain:
- clean voice track
- music track
- SFX track
- mixed master
